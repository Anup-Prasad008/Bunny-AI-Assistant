# Bunny AI – User Data Deletion ## How to Delete Your Account and Personal Data Bunny AI respects your privacy and gives users the right to request deletion of their personal information. ### How to request data deletion To request deletion of your Bunny AI account and associated personal data: 1. Send an email to our support email address. 2. Use the subject: **Bunny AI – Account and Data Deletion Request**. 3. Include the email address associated with your Bunny AI account. 4. Mention that you want your account and personal data deleted. ### What information will be deleted? Depending on the data stored by Bunny AI, deletion may include: - Account information - Name and email address - Facebook Login information and associated identifiers - Profile information - Associated user data stored by Bunny AI ### Processing time We will review your request and process eligible deletion requests within 30 days. ### Facebook Login You can also remove Bunny AI from your Facebook account by visiting Facebook Settings → Apps and Websites → Bunny AI → Remove. Removing the Facebook connection does not automatically guarantee deletion of data stored separately by Bunny AI. Please contact us to request that deletion. ### Contact For account deletion and privacy-related questions, contact: **Support Email:** YOUR-ACTUAL-SUPPORT-EMAIL
